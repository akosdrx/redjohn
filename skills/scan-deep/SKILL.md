# Deep scan profile

Establish standard and high coverage, then investigate unresolved trust boundaries and novel hypotheses. Compare code paths, patches and versions; perform targeted fuzzing only when the engagement supplies a safe harness and explicit bounds. Do not expand to adjacent services, third-party dependencies, cluster nodes or real-user data. Deep depth never permits destructive operations, persistence or unbounded load.

All numbered surface skills may be selected when their preconditions and scope match (including 05-attack-surface-planner, 25-waf-edge-posture, 32-mfa-captcha-audit); 75-zeroday-research is for evidence-supported novel hypotheses, not speculative vulnerability claims. The coordinator tracks negative coverage, integrates specialist results, independently replays findings and assembles a reproducible report through 90-evidence-reporting. State any live-test or operator-validation gaps.

This profile is inherited by all descendants. Load the master methodology and relevant surface skills with builtin:load_skill. Read referenced files with builtin:read_skill_file. Finish only with evidence references, negative coverage and clear limitations.
